# Income Tracker (Notion Template) - $9

Track income, expenses, and monthly totals in one simple Notion page. Great for freelancers, creators, and side hustlers.

## Build spec

### 1. Transactions (database)
Properties:
- Title (title)
- Type (select: Income, Expense)
- Amount (number, $)
- Category (select: Client work, Product, Subscription, Tools, Other)
- Date (date)
- Source (text)

Views:
- Table all, sorted by Date (newest first)
- Board grouped by Type
- Calendar by Date

### 2. Monthly summary
Use a linked database view filtered by current month, plus rollup/formula cards:
- Total income (sum of Income amounts)
- Total expenses (sum of Expense amounts)
- Net (income - expenses)

### 3. Goals
Simple checklist or progress bar toward a monthly income target.
