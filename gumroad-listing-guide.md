# Gumroad Listing Guide

How to list and sell the Notion templates in this repo on Gumroad.

## 1. Prepare the template
- Build the template in Notion from its spec file.
- Click Share -> publish to web, and enable "Allow duplicate as template."
- Copy the public template link.

## 2. Create the Gumroad product
1. Sign up at gumroad.com (free; they take a small fee per sale).
2. New product -> Digital product.
3. Name it (e.g., "Freelancer Dashboard - Notion Template").
4. Set the price (see README table: $9-$19).
5. In the content section, deliver the Notion duplicate link (and a short PDF setup guide).

## 3. Make the listing convert
- Cover image: a clean screenshot of the template.
- 3-5 gallery images showing each view.
- Description: problem it solves, what's inside, who it's for.
- Add a few bullet benefits at the top.

## 4. Drive traffic (free)
- Post screenshots/demos on X, Reddit (r/Notion), and TikTok.
- Offer a free mini-version as a lead magnet.
- Bundle all 3 templates at a discount.

## 5. Pricing strategy
| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| Income Tracker | $9 |
| Project Planner | $15 |
| Freelancer Dashboard | $19 |
| All-3 Bundle | $29 |

Keep the entry price low to build reviews, then upsell the bundle.
